Overview

The BGPro WCAM pack brings Indian Railways’ workhorse dual-power locomotives to Microsoft Train Simulator and Open Rails. WCAM classes ran on both AC and DC electrification across the Central and Western Railway networks, and this pack recreates their distinct bodywork, pantographs, sounds and physics with BGPro’s trademark attention to detail. Drop-in ready for your Indian route collections.

Pack Highlights

How to Install

Follow these steps for a safe, clean setup on Windows.

  1. Download the Pack — Use either the OpenCloud or MediaFire link below.
  2. Extract — Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip — right-click the archive and extract here.
  3. Copy to Trainset — Move the extracted loco folder into .../Train Simulator/TRAINS/TRAINSET/.
  4. Drive It — Launch MSTS or Open Rails, pick your route and couple up the WCAM.
