About This Route

The Konkan Coastal Link — Vasco Update is a faithful recreation of the Konkan Railway coastal line running to the port town of Vasco da Gama in Goa — one of the most scenic engineering marvels on the Indian network.

The coastal link threads the Western Ghats down to the Arabian Sea, so every run is a mix of long tunnels, tall viaducts, river crossings and expresses such as the Konkan Kanya and Jan Shatabdi gliding through the green Sahyadri landscape.

This Vasco update refreshes the route with improved scenery, updated textures and smoother performance, making it one of the most atmospheric Indian coastal routes available for MSTS / Open Rails.

Key Features

  • Vasco da Gama — the historic port terminus in Goa where the coastal line meets the harbour, alive with passenger expresses and iron-ore freights.
  • Konkan Coastal Corridor — the legendary sea-hugging line through tunnels, viaducts and the lush green valleys of the Sahyadri hills.
  • Scenic Coastal Operations — express trains and monsoon-soaked runs along the Arabian Sea, crossing rivers and backwaters along the way.
  • Refreshed Scenery — improved station detail, updated textures, smoother performance and authentic Konkan coastline ambience.

Route Description

The Konkan Coastal Link — Vasco Update recreates the Konkan Railway coastal line into Vasco da Gama in painstaking detail for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails. This is one of the most scenic corridors on the Indian network — the great sea-side highway where mountains, rivers and the Arabian Sea meet.

Trains wind through endless tunnels and viaducts as premium expresses like the Konkan Kanya share the single line with freight rakes heading for the port. Dense coastal traffic and dramatic monsoon scenery make every run feel alive.

This Vasco update brings improved scenery, refreshed textures and smoother performance, with realistic signalling, yards, stations and level crossings. It is perfect for scenic express runs as well as relaxed coastal freight driving — a complete and authentic Konkan Railway experience.
