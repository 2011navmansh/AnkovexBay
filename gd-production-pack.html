Compatibility

  • Runs on Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails.
  • Windows — addon folders are dropped into .../Train Simulator/TRAINS/TRAINSET/.
  • Wagon folders contain .wag, .eng and .s files used by both simulators.
  • Consists are built with ConBuilder, Consist Editor or the Open Rails consist creator.

How to Install the GD Production Pack

Easy step-by-step setup to install rolling stock into Train Simulator & Open Rails.

  1. Extract Addon Folder — Extract the zip archive to get the addon folder (containing .wag, .eng, .s files).
  2. Copy to TRAINSET — Place the extracted folder into .../Train Simulator/TRAINS/TRAINSET/.
  3. Create Consist (.con) — Use ConBuilder, Consist Editor, or Open Rails consist creator to attach the wagons to your trains.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — Launch simulator, pick your new consist and hit the rails!
