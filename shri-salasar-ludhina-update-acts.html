About These Activities

The SHRI SALASAR LUDHINA UPDATE ACTS pack collects sixteen ready-to-run scenario files for the Shri Salasar Route following the Ludhiana Junction update. The set covers express, Shatabdi, Duronto and Humsafar workings alongside dedicated shunting and freight duties, giving the route a full spread of operations to drive.

Each activity is supplied as a single RAR or ZIP archive. Download the scenario you want, extract it, and copy the activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of your Shri Salasar route to start driving.

How to Load Shri Salasar Ludhina Update Acts

Quick 4-step guide to get any activity running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

  1. Download the Activity File — choose the activity you want in the table below and save the archive to your PC.
  2. Extract — right-click the RAR or ZIP and extract with WinRAR or 7-Zip into a folder.
  3. Copy to the Route Folder — move the extracted activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of your Shri Salasar route in .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the activity and start your run.
