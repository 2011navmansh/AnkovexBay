Quick 4-Step Guide

How to Run MSTS Activities

Quick 4-step guide to load any route activity inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

01
map

Install the Route

First make sure the route you want is installed from the Routes page — download and extract into .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.

02
edit_note

Open the Activity Editor

Launch the MSTS Activity Editor or Open Rails and pick your route from the list.

03
playlist_add

Choose or Build an Activity

Select a ready activity or create a new scenario with your own consists, traffic and timing.

04
sports_esports

Drive & Enjoy

Start the activity, follow the timetable and run your train along the route. Enjoy the session!
