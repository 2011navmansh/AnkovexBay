How to Make an MSTS Loop Horn

  1. Download & install — grab the archive above, extract it and install REAPER on your PC.
  2. Get a horn sample — record a clean loco horn clip (or reuse a free WAV sample) and import it into REAPER.
  3. Clean & loop — trim noise at the edges, find a seamless loop point on the sustain of the horn and enable loop playback until it sounds continuous.
  4. Render — render the looped section as a plain 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV named exactly like your loco's horn file.
  5. Drop into MSTS — replace the horn WAV inside your loco folder (.../TRAINS/TRAINSET/<loco>/SOUND) or the carriers' SOUND folder, and fire up MSTS or Open Rails.
info

Good to know: MSTS loco files reference horn WAVs by name in the eng/wag sound blocks — keep the file names identical so the sim picks your new loop horn automatically.
