About AJ.EOG.V3.SOUND

AJ.EOG.V3.SOUND is a realistic sound pack built for Microsoft Train Simulator & Open Rails. It focuses on the audio that defines a run — the engine note under load, the horn answering a curve, and the traction hum as the loco pulls away.

Everything ships in one compact 7z archive, so you can download it, extract it and drop the files into your sound setup without a long install chain. It is free, with no sign-up and no payment.

How to Install

  1. Download — Open the download page and grab the 7z archive.
  2. Extract — Extract the archive with 7-Zip / WinRAR.
  3. Copy to Sounds — Copy the sound files into your train's \SOUND\ folder.
  4. Match Names — Make sure the .eng file references the sound names included in the pack.
  5. Launch & Listen — Open MSTS or Open Rails and test the horn, engine and traction audio from the cab.
