About This Route

The Konkan Coastal Link — Kulem Update extends the coastal link inland from the Konkan shore to Kulem (QLM) in Goa — a small but vital station on the Guntakal–Vasco da Gama section, operated by the South Western Railway (Hubli division).

Kulem sits at 77 m elevation with 3 platforms and 5 tracks, and is famous as the point where banker locomotives are attached before trains tackle the steep climb past Dudhsagar Falls to Castle Rock through the Western Ghats.

This update refreshes the whole link with improved scenery, updated textures and smoother performance — the Goa Express, Poorna Express, Vasco–Chennai Express, Amaravati Express and the Vasco–Kulem shuttle all run through here.

Route Highlights

  • Kulem (QLM) — the update's new terminus in Goa, where banker engines couple on for the ghat climb towards Castle Rock.
  • Dudhsagar Ghat Section — the legendary climb past the Dudhsagar Falls with deep cuttings, tunnels and towering viaducts.
  • Coastal to Ghats Blend — sea-level Konkan curves running into Western Ghats gradients, tunnels and river crossings in one route.
  • Refreshed Scenery — updated textures, denser foliage, monsoon ambience and smoother performance across the whole link.

How to Install Konkan Coastal Link — Kulem Update

Quick 4-step guide to get the Konkan Coastal Link — Kulem Update running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 7 split RAR parts (~14.1 GB).

  1. Download All 7 Parts — grab every part from the table above, save all 7 RAR parts into one folder, then right-click part01 and extract with WinRAR or 7-Zip.
