About This Route

The Western_railways_v12_route is the V12 edition of the Western Railways route for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails, covering the Kota–Mathura trunk section of the Delhi–Mumbai corridor through Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh.

From Kota Junction the line crosses the Chambal river and runs north through Sawai Madhopur, Gangapur City, Hindaun City and Bayana to Mathura Junction — one of the busiest and most characterful stretches of Indian Railways' broad-gauge network.

The route ships as 4 split ZIP parts (~6.3 GB): download every part into one folder, then extract Part 1 and the archive merges automatically.

Key Features

  • Kota–Mathura Trunk — Kota Junction to Mathura Junction via Sawai Madhopur, Gangapur City and Bayana.
  • Updated Trackwork — refined track, turnouts and block sections across the Delhi–Mumbai corridor.
  • Fresh Scenery & Textures — a new scenery and texture pass along the route.
  • Traffic Variety — long-distance expresses, passenger workings and freight action.
  • MSTS + Open Rails Ready — built for Microsoft Train Simulator and fully compatible with Open Rails on Windows.

How to Install Western_railways_v12_route

Quick 4-step guide to get Western_railways_v12_route running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 4 split ZIP parts (~6.3 GB).

  1. Download & Extract — download all 4 ZIP parts into the same folder, then right-click Part 1 → Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip (the parts merge automatically).
  2. Copy to Routes Folder — move the extracted route folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  3. Run Installme.bat — double-click installme.bat inside the route folder to copy required default assets (if included).
  4. Launch & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the Western Railways route, and start your session!
