About This Route

The NORTHEN DIVINE PATHANKOT UPDATE is the final update to the Northern Divine Route for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails — a Northern Railway route that brings the Amritsar, Jalandhar, Ludhiana and Pathankot corridors of Punjab to life.

This update completes the route with the Pathankot Junction to Jalandhar Cantt Junction section, adding Unchi Bassi, Mirthal and Mukerian to the network. The earlier Amritsar–Jalandhar–Ludhiana mainline and the Amritsar–Batala–Gurdaspur–Pathankot branch are also part of the route, giving you a wide spread of Northern Railway operations to explore.

Route Coverage

  • Final Phase — Pathankot Jn → Pathankot Cantt → Unchi Bassi → Mirthal → Mukerian → Jalandhar Cantt Jn.
  • Mainline — Amritsar Jn → Jalandhar City Jn → Jalandhar Cantt → Ludhiana Jn.
  • Branch Line — Amritsar Jn → Verka Jn → Batala Jn → Dhariwal → Gurdaspur → Dinanagar → Bharoli Jn → Pathankot Jn.

Key Features

  • Completed Network — the final Pathankot–Jalandhar Cantt extension ties the route together.
  • Updated Scenery — refreshed trackwork, station environments and Punjab countryside detail.
  • Northern Railway Atmosphere — busy junctions, local passenger services and mainline expresses.
  • Split Archive — 3 RAR parts that merge automatically when you extract Part 1.

Route Description

From the temple city of Amritsar through the industrial belt of Jalandhar and Ludhiana, out to the foothills at Pathankot, the Northern Divine Route captures the everyday rhythm of Punjab's rail network. The final update pushes the line from Pathankot Junction towards Jalandhar Cantt, opening up a complete through run across the region.

How to Install NORTHEN DIVINE PATHANKOT UPDATE

Quick 4-step guide to get the NORTHEN DIVINE PATHANKOT UPDATE running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 3 split RAR parts.

  1. Download & Extract — download all 3 RAR parts into the same folder, then right-click Part 1 → Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip (the parts merge automatically).
  2. Copy to Routes Folder — move the route folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  3. Run Installme.bat — double-click installme.bat inside the route folder to copy required default assets.
  4. Launch & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the newly added route, and start your session!
