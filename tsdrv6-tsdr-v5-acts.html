About These Activities

The TSDRV6 + TSDR V5 pack collects the ready-to-run scenario files built for the TSD Route V6 and TSD Route V5 — 45 activities covering the Southern and South Central corridors around Chennai, Tirupati, Bengaluru, Mysuru, Secunderabad and Mumbai.

From prestigious day trains like the 12028 KSR Bengaluru–MGR Chennai Central Shatabdi and 12006 Shatabdi to long-haul runs such as the 22504 Dibrugarh–Kanniyakumari Vivek Express, 16331 Mumbai CSMT–Thiruvananthapuram Express and 22691 KSR Bengaluru–Hazrat Nizamuddin Rajdhani, every file downloads as a single archive you can extract and load straight into MSTS or Open Rails.

Shorter duties are covered too — 66524 Hindupur–KSR Bengaluru MEMU, 43403 MASS–AJJ EMU and the Kacheguda–Guntakal Passenger — along with specials, double-decker and double-duty workings.

Works with Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) & Open Rails on Windows.

  • Requires the TSD Route V6 or TSD Route V5 — each activity loads from the route's ACTIVITIES folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  • Activity progress is saved in the route's ACTIVITIES and SERVICES folders, so keep the extracted files together.

How to Load TSDRV6 + TSDR V5 Activities

Quick 4-step guide to get any activity running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

  1. Download the Activity File — hit the Download button on the activity you want in the table below and save the archive to your PC.
  2. Extract — right-click the archive and extract with WinRAR, 7-Zip or Windows Explorer into a folder.
  3. Copy to the Route Folder — move the extracted activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of your TSD Route V6 or TSDR V5 installation in .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the activity and start your run. Enjoy the session!
