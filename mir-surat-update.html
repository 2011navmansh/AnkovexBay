About This Route

The MIR Surat Update is the Surat-focused release of Mega Indian Railways (MIR) for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails. Surat (ST) is one of Western Railway's busiest stations and a key stop on the Mumbai–Delhi trunk, handling everything from Rajdhani and Vande Bharat expresses to a constant flow of passenger and freight traffic.

This update refreshes the Surat station complex and its approaches with updated trackwork, platforms, yards, overhead equipment and surrounding scenery — giving the section a cleaner, more detailed and more authentic Western Railway feel.

Key Features

  • Surat Station Complex — detailed platforms, station buildings, foot overbridges and the busy yard layout that makes Surat such a recognisable junction.
  • Western Railway Mainline — electrified broad-gauge running with OHE, signals and the dense traffic atmosphere of the Mumbai–Delhi corridor.
  • Updated Scenery & Textures — refreshed surroundings and station details for a more polished run through the Surat area.
  • MSTS + Open Rails Ready — built for Microsoft Train Simulator and fully compatible with Open Rails on Windows.
  • Split RAR Archive — 4 parts that merge automatically when you extract Part 1.

Route Description

Surat sits at the heart of the Western Railway's Mumbai–Delhi mainline, where long-distance expresses, suburban-style locals and heavy goods trains share one of India's most densely worked corridors. The MIR Surat Update brings that atmosphere into MSTS and Open Rails with a refreshed recreation of the station and its immediate approaches.

How to Install the MIR Surat Update

Quick 4-step guide to get the MIR Surat Update running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 4 split RAR parts (~6.3 GB).

  1. Download & Extract — download all 4 RAR parts into the same folder, then right-click Part 1 → Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip (the parts merge automatically).
  2. Copy to Routes Folder — move the route folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  3. Run Installme.bat — double-click installme.bat inside the route folder to copy required default assets (if included).
  4. Launch & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the MIR route, and start your session from Surat!
