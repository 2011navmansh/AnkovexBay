About These Activities

The WRV12 Activities pack collects the ready-to-run scenario files built for the Western Railways V12 route — 57 activities covering premium day trains, long-haul mail expresses and weekly superfast runs through Hazrat Nizamuddin, Kota, Nagda, Ratlam, Vadodara and Mumbai Central.

Drive flagship trains like the 12002 New Delhi–Rani Kamalapati Shatabdi, 20172 NZM–Rani Kamalapati Vande Bharat Express and 22222 Hazrat Nizamuddin–Mumbai CSMT Rajdhani, take the 12964 Mewar SF Express and 12976 Jaipur–Mysuru Express through the hills, or work freight-connected stretches on the 12909 Kota–NZM Garib Rath and 12904 Golden Temple Mail Part 1.

Every file downloads as a single RAR you can extract and load straight into MSTS or Open Rails — no installer needed.

  • Activity progress is saved in the route's ACTIVITIES and SERVICES folders, so keep the extracted files together.
  • Two packs (16335 GIMB–NCJ SF and 22632 Anuvrat Express) require the patch/fix included in their own folder — install it first, then drive.

How to Load WRV12 Activities

Quick 4-step guide to get any Western Railways V12 activity running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

  1. Download the Activity File — hit the Download button on the activity you want in the table below and save the .rar file to your PC.
  2. Extract — right-click the archive and extract with WinRAR or 7-Zip into a folder.
  3. Copy to the Route Folder — move the extracted activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of your Western Railways V12 route in .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the activity and start your run. Enjoy the session!
