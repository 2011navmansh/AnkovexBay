Overview

BGPro_ICF_GR is part of the BG Pro Production line-up — a dedicated ICF coach pack for Microsoft Train Simulator and Open Rails. Build authentic Indian Railways rakes with detailed coach bodies, realistic interiors and classic ICF liveries, ideal for express, mail and passenger services on any route.

The pack ships as a single 7z archive, so setup is quick: extract, copy the coach folders into your TRAINSET directory and build your consist. Free to download, no sign-up and no payment.
