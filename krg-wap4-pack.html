How to Install the KRG WAP4 Pack

Easy step-by-step setup to install the split archive into Train Simulator & Open Rails.

  1. Download Both Parts — Download Part 001 and Part 002 into the same folder on your PC.
  2. Extract Part 001 — Right-click MSTS-bddkd5rwba.zip.001 → Extract Here (7-Zip / WinRAR will auto-join .002).
  3. Copy to TRAINSET — Place the extracted loco folder into .../Train Simulator/TRAINS/TRAINSET/.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — Launch simulator, choose your new WAP-4 and hit Notch 8!
