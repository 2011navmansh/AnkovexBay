How to Install BGPro Coaches

Easy step-by-step setup to add any BG Pro Production ICF coach pack to Train Simulator & Open Rails.

  1. Download & Extract — Grab a pack above and extract it with 7-Zip or WinRAR.
  2. Open the Pack Folder — Open the extracted folder containing the ICF coach folders.
  3. Copy to Trainset — Copy the coach folders into your \TRAINSET\ directory of MSTS or Open Rails.
  4. Consist & Run — Build your rake in the Activity Editor / Open Rails consist creator and hit the rails.
