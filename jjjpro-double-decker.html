About JJJPro_Double_Decker

JJJPro_Double_Decker is the much-awaited Indian Railways Double Decker pack for MSTS & Open Rails, modelled in 3D by Jayrajsinh Jadeja of JJJ Productions. The pack brings the real Double Decker rake — the same style seen on services like the Ahmedabad–Mumbai Double Decker — into your simulator, with a full-length rake built for daylight intercity runs.

Every coach is built around the real Double Decker profile: the stretched upper and lower deck window bands, the gangway ends, the underframe equipment and the correct coach ends. It ships with both official-style livery sets plus advertising wraps and a matching EOG car, so you can run an authentic short rake straight out of the box. Drop it into \TRAINSET\, build a consist and the rake is ready — no extra setup needed.

  • Highly Detailed 3D Model — Precisely modelled Double Decker body, window bands, gangways and underframe detail.

How to Install

  1. Download & Extract — Download the ZIP and extract it with WinRAR / 7-Zip.
  2. Open the Pack Folder — Open the extracted folder containing the Double Decker coach folders.
  3. Copy to Trainset — Copy the coach folders into your \TRAINSET\ directory of MSTS or Open Rails.
  4. Consist & Run — Build a Double Decker consist (with the EOG car at the end) and hit the rails.
