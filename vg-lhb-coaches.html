Overview

VG LHB Coaches brings Varun Ganesh's (VG) high-fidelity LHB coach models to Microsoft Train Simulator and Open Rails. The pack focuses on realistic interiors, accurate coach bodies and HD detailing, so an LHB rake looks right from the platform as well as from inside the cabin.

The pack ships as a two-part split archive totalling roughly 2.8 GB. Download both RAR parts into the same folder, extract Part 1 with WinRAR or 7-Zip, then copy the coach folders into your TRAINSET directory and build your consist.

How to Install VG LHB Coaches

  1. Download Both Parts — save VG_LHB_COACHES.part1.rar and VG_LHB_COACHES.part2.rar into the same folder.
  2. Extract Part 1 — right-click Part 1 → Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip (the parts merge automatically).
  3. Copy to Trainset — copy the coach folders into your \TRAINSET\ directory of MSTS or Open Rails.
  4. Consist & Run — build an LHB rake in the Activity Editor / Open Rails consist creator and hit the rails.
warning

Note: Download both RAR parts — the archive will not open with only one. Extraction requires WinRAR or 7-Zip.
