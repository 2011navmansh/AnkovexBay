Step by Step

How to Install Sound Packs

Quick 4-step guide to get any sound pack running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

  1. Download & Extract — Download the zip/rar archive and extract using WinRAR or 7-Zip.
  2. Run installme.bat — Double-click installme.bat to copy the .s sound files into \SOUNDS\ and pull required default assets.
  3. Match Sound Names — Engine .eng files must reference the exact sound names listed in the pack's README.
  4. Launch & Listen — Open MSTS or Open Rails and test horn, brake and engine sounds from the cab.
