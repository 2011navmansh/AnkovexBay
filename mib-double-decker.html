About MIB_Double_Decker

MIB_Double_Decker is a detailed Indian Railways Double Decker coach pack for MSTS & Open Rails. The pack ships seven body liveries — Canara (NWR), Green (WR), Nippon (SR), Ramraj (SWR), Uday (SWR), plain Red and a weathered Red NWR variant — so you can build authentic daytime intercity rakes with the right advertisement wraps.

Every coach follows the real Double Decker profile: the stretched upper and lower deck window bands, gangway ends, underframe equipment and correct coach ends. Interior, seat and glass textures are included, along with a dedicated Night folder for evening runs. The pack also carries a complete sound set with speed-based rolling sounds, coupling audio and LHB/EOG sound files — drop the coach folder into \TRAINSET\, build a consist and the rake is ready.
