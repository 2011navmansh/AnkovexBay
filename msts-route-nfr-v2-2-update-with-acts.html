About This Route

The NFR Version 2.2 Update with Acts is a refreshed recreation of Northeast Frontier Railway's Guwahati–New Jalpaiguri corridor for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails. The route threads through Lower Assam and North Bengal, following the Brahmaputra valley before crossing the tea gardens, forests and river plains that lead toward Siliguri.

Version 2.2 brings updated trackwork, station scenery and textures across the corridor, along with a ready-to-run activity pack bundled into the same ZIP download. The activities cover passenger, express and freight workings so you can start driving straight after installation.

The line is one of the most important in the Indian network — the entire Northeast connects to the rest of the country through the narrow Siliguri chicken's-neck corridor. From the busy platforms of Guwahati and the pilgrim hub of Kamakhya, the route runs west through Rangiya and New Bongaigaon, across the Assam–Bengal border at Alipurduar and New Cooch Behar, and on to New Jalpaiguri at the foot of the Himalayas.

Route Description

The Guwahati–New Jalpaiguri route is the railway lifeline of India's Northeast. Starting from Guwahati Junction, trains pass the sacred Kamakhya shrine before heading west along the Brahmaputra valley through Rangiya and the busy junction at New Bongaigaon. Beyond the Assam border, the line crosses the rolling tea country and forest belts of North Bengal via Alipurduar and New Cooch Behar, finally reaching New Jalpaiguri and the Siliguri gateway.

The version 2.2 update sharpens the whole run with improved scenery, refreshed textures and smoother performance, while the bundled activities add purpose to the route — from express runs and local passenger services to heavy freight hauls. It is a complete Northeast Frontier Railway experience for MSTS and Open Rails.

How to Install NFR Version 2.2 Update with Acts

Quick 4-step guide to get the NFR Version 2.2 Update with Acts running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 9 split ZIP parts (~14.5 GB) and includes the activity pack.

  1. Download All 9 Parts — save every .zip.001 to .zip.009 file into the same folder.
  2. Extract — right-click MSTS-bawqgc6tu72hbca.zip.001 and extract with 7-Zip or WinRAR. The numbered parts merge automatically.
  3. Copy to Routes Folder — move the extracted route folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/, then run installme.bat inside the route folder.
  4. Launch & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the NFR route, and pick any of the included activities from the ACTIVITIES folder.
