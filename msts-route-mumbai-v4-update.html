About This Route

The Mumbai V4 Update Freeware Route is a refreshed edition of the classic Mumbai route for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails. It brings India's busiest suburban network back to the simulator with updated trackwork, station detailing and scenery across the Western Railway, Central Railway and Harbour line corridors.

From the grand terminals of Mumbai CSMT and Mumbai Central, the route spreads through Dadar, Kurla, Thane, Panvel and the dense city suburbs — a constant flow of local EMUs, long-distance expresses and freight traffic that defines Mumbai's railway atmosphere.

The V4 update focuses on a cleaner, smoother and more authentic driving experience while keeping the route fully freeware for the MSTS and Open Rails community.

Key Features

  • Mumbai CSMT & Mumbai Central — the route's iconic terminal stations, with their busy platforms, yard approaches and heritage architecture.
  • Suburban Network — Western Railway, Central Railway and Harbour line sections with local EMU operations and dense stop-start running.
  • Busy Junctions — Dadar, Kurla, Thane and Panvel, with converging lines, flyovers, yards and constant traffic.
  • V4 Refresh — updated trackwork, signalling, platforms, station buildings and surrounding city scenery.
  • MSTS + Open Rails Ready — built for Microsoft Train Simulator and fully compatible with Open Rails on Windows.

Route Description

Mumbai's suburban railway is the lifeline of India's financial capital, carrying millions of passengers every day across fast and slow corridors. The Mumbai V4 Update recreates that world for MSTS and Open Rails — from the diesel and electric loco-hauled expresses at the terminal stations to the packed EMU locals threading through the city.

Drive suburban services through Churchgate – Mumbai Central – Dadar – Bandra – Andheri – Borivali, explore the Central Railway side through CSMT – Dadar – Kurla – Thane – Kalyan, or take the Harbour line toward Panvel. Mainline expresses and freight services add variety, with realistic signalling, station layouts and city scenery throughout.

How to Install the Mumbai V4 Update

Quick 4-step guide to get the Mumbai V4 Update running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 3 split ZIP parts (~4.29 GB).

  1. Download & Extract — download all 3 ZIP parts into the same folder, then right-click Part 1 → Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip (the parts merge automatically).
  2. Copy to Routes Folder — move the route folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  3. Run Installme.bat — double-click installme.bat inside the route folder to copy required default assets (if included).
  4. Launch & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the Mumbai route, and start your session!
