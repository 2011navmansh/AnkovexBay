Our Story · Since 2026: born from the frustration of sketchy mirrors and endless download walls — built into the clean, fast, totally free bay we always wanted.

Hey there, fellow gamers! Pull up a chair — let us tell you about Ankovex Bay. It started with the story every one of us knows:

Picture this: it's 2026, and we're huddled around a computer, cursing at yet another sketchy download that's taking forever — and is probably packed with more viruses than a hospital ward. Sound familiar?

Every train-sim enthusiast we know had the same version of that story: hunting for a single Indian MSTS route across ten sketchy mirrors, half of them broken, the rest demanding endless "steps" to reach one file.

That's when we decided to build the platform we'd always dreamed of — a place where downloading games and MSTS addons doesn't feel like navigating a minefield while wearing a blindfold.

Fast forward to 2026: Ankovex Bay was born. Today it's home to pre-installed games and a growing, hand-picked library of Indian MSTS routes, locomotives, sounds and mods — all in one clean, fast and totally free bay.

Welcome to Ankovex Bay. Game on, friends!
