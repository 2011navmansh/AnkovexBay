Overview

FluxDown is a modern, no-nonsense download manager built for speed. It splits every file into multiple threads to saturate your connection, then reassembles everything seamlessly. Pause and resume any download, schedule transfers for off-peak hours, and capture downloads straight from your browser with the official extension. Because it is fully open source and ad-free, what you see is exactly what you get — a clean, fast downloader that respects you.
