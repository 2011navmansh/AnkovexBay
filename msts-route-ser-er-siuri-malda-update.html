About This Route

The SER & ER Siuri Malda Update is a refreshed route release for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails, covering the Siuri–Malda belt of West Bengal where South Eastern and Eastern Railway lines meet the state's busy eastern corridors.

This update ships as 6 split RAR parts and focuses on a cleaner, more detailed run: refreshed trackwork, improved station scenery and updated textures throughout the route, from the Birbhum countryside around Siuri to the approach into Malda Town.

Key Features

  • Siuri–Malda Corridor — a West Bengal route linking the Birbhum district with Malda Town and its surrounding railway network.
  • Updated Trackwork & Signalling — refreshed permanent way and signalling for smoother, more authentic operations.
  • Station Scenery — detailed platforms, station buildings and lineside scenery across the route.
  • Refreshed Textures — updated ground, track and scenery textures for a more polished look.
  • Split RAR Archive — 6 parts that merge automatically when you extract Part 1.
  • MSTS + Open Rails Ready — built for Microsoft Train Simulator and fully compatible with Open Rails on Windows.

Route Description

Siuri sits in the heart of Birbhum district, while Malda Town anchors the busy rail network of north-central West Bengal. The SER & ER Siuri Malda Update brings that stretch into MSTS and Open Rails with a refreshed recreation of the corridor — passenger services, freight traffic and the everyday rhythm of Eastern India's railways.

How to Install the SER & ER Siuri Malda Update

Quick 4-step guide to get the SER & ER Siuri Malda Update running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 6 split RAR parts (~10.4 GB).

  1. Download & Extract — download all 6 RAR parts into the same folder, then right-click Part 1 → Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip (the parts merge automatically).
  2. Copy to Routes Folder — move the route folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  3. Run Installme.bat — double-click installme.bat inside the route folder to copy required default assets (if included).
  4. Launch & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the newly added route, and start your session!
