About the MGW Library

The MGW Sounds collection brings together 19 hand-tuned Indian locomotive sound packs for Microsoft Train Simulator & Open Rails — WAP-7, WAP-5, WAP-4, WAG-9, WDP-4D and WDG-4D horn & sound sets along with complete ICF and LHB coach audio. Every pack below downloads free — no sign-up, no payment.

Collection Highlights

  • 19 hand-tuned Indian locomotive sound & horn packs
  • WAP-7 versions from v1.2 to V15, plus dual steam horns
  • WAP-5, WAP-4, WAG-9, WDP-4D & WDG-4D engine & horn sets
  • Complete ICF and LHB coach sound libraries
  • Free downloads — no sign-up

Compatibility

  • Runs on Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails.
  • Windows.
  • Every pack is a free ZIP, RAR or 7z archive — extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip.

How to Install

  1. Pick a Pack — Open any pack below and hit its download button to grab the free archive.
  2. Extract — Extract the ZIP / RAR / 7z with WinRAR or 7-Zip.
  3. Copy to Sounds Folder — Copy the .sms / .wav files into your engine's \SOUNDS\ folder.
  4. Match Sound Names — Point your .eng files to the exact sound names listed in the pack's README.
  5. Launch & Listen — Open MSTS or Open Rails and test horn, traction and brake sounds from the cab.
