Step by Step

How to Install MSTS Locomotives

Easy step-by-step setup to install locomotives into Train Simulator & Open Rails.

  1. Extract Engine Folder — Extract the zip archive to get the engine folder (containing .eng, .wag, .s files).
  2. Copy to TRAINSET — Place the extracted loco folder into .../Train Simulator/TRAINS/TRAINSET/.
  3. Create Consist (.con) — Use ConBuilder, Consist Editor, or Open Rails consist creator to attach coaches or wagons.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — Launch simulator, choose your new locomotive and hit Notch 8!
