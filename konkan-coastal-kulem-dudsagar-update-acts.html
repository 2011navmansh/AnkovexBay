About These Activities

The Konkan Coastal Kulem (Dudhsagar Update) activity pack brings ready-to-run scenarios to the Konkan Coastal Link — Kulem Update route — the coastal run pushed inland to Kulem (QLM) on the Guntakal–Vasco da Gama section of South Western Railway.

Every act is built around the real working of this legendary ghat section — banker-assisted climbs past Dudhsagar Falls, ghat tunnels, deep cuttings and river crossings between Vasco da Gama, Madgaon and Kulem.

The pack contains 4 activity files (ZIP / RAR) — mainline expresses, a long-distance interzone link and a community-built edition — all loadable in Microsoft Train Simulator and Open Rails.

Pack Description

  • 12779 — Goa Express — the classic Goa Express duty over the coastal line and the Kulem ghat, with banker assistance on the Dudhsagar climb.
  • 17040 — Vasco da Gama–Secunderabad Express — long interzone express worked over the ghat gradients from the Goan coast towards the Deccan.
  • 17321 — Vasco da Gama–Jasidih Express — the Vasco–Jasidih link running inland through the tunnels and viaducts of the ghat section.
  • 17321 — Community Edition (by Alok) — a community-tuned variant of the 17321 working, built by Alok for the Kulem Update.

How to Load Konkan Coastal Kulem Activities

Quick 4-step guide to get any Konkan Coastal Link (Kulem — Dudhsagar Update) activity running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

  1. Download the Activity File — use the Download button in the table above. Save the .zip / .rar archive to your PC.
  2. Extract — right-click the archive and extract with WinRAR or 7-Zip into a folder.
  3. Copy to the Route Folder — move the extracted activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of your Konkan Coastal Link — Kulem route in .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the activity and start your Kulem ghat run past Dudhsagar. Enjoy the session!
