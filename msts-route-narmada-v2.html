About This Route

The Narmada V2 Route is a detailed recreation of West Central Railway's corridor through the heart of the Narmada valley for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails. It brings the classic Central India mainline to the simulator — a line that threads along the sacred Narmada river through Pipariya, Gadarwara and Narsinghpur before reaching the historic junction city of Jabalpur.

From the endless crossing movements of Itarsi Junction (ET), one of India's busiest railway crossroads, the route rolls across the Satpura plateau through forests, river bridges and quiet mofussil stations toward Jabalpur (JBP) — home of the Marble Rocks and the mighty Dhuandhar falls on the Narmada. Beyond Jabalpur, the line continues through Katni, Maihar and Satna, carrying a constant mix of heavy freight and long-distance expresses.

Fully signalled with realistic block sections, stations, yards and level crossings, the route is perfect for running Duronto, Garib Rath and Mail/Express services as well as the coal, cement and container freights that make the Jabalpur division one of West Central Railway's busiest stretches.

Key Features

  • Itarsi Junction (ET) — the great Central India crossroads where the Mumbai–Delhi trunk meets the Jabalpur line, with vast yards and non-stop traffic.
  • Jabalpur (JBP) — the Narmada valley's premier station, with Marble Rocks and Dhuandhar falls scenery along the river.
  • Narmada Valley Mainline — Pipariya, Gadarwara and Narsinghpur flanked by forests, river bridges and Satpura hill country.
  • Onward to Katni & Satna — the westward push through Katni, Maihar and Satna Jn with heavy freight and express action.
  • MSTS + Open Rails Ready — built for Microsoft Train Simulator and fully compatible with Open Rails on Windows.

Route Description

The Narmada is the lifeline of Central India, and the railway that follows its valley is one of the most characterful mainlines in the country. The Narmada V2 Route recreates that world — from the constant crossing movements at Itarsi to the river ghats and forests around Jabalpur, and on through the Katni and Satna freights that keep the West Central Railway moving day and night.

Drive long-distance expresses across the valley, work heavy coal and cement freights over the Satpura gradients, or explore the stations and yards at your own pace in Explore mode. The route ships as 4 split RAR parts: download all four, keep them together in one folder, and extract Part 1 — the archive merges automatically.

How to Install the Narmada V2 Route

Quick 4-step guide to get the Narmada V2 Route running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 4 split RAR parts (~6.03 GB).

  1. Download & Extract — download all 4 RAR parts into the same folder, then right-click Part 1 → Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip (the parts merge automatically).
  2. Copy to Routes Folder — move the route folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  3. Run Installme.bat — double-click installme.bat inside the route folder to copy required default assets (if included).
  4. Launch & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the Narmada route, and start your session!
