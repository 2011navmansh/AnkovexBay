About These Activities

The TSCR-V2-KZJ-UPDATE_ACTS pack collects the ready-to-run scenario files built for the TSCR-V2-KZJ update — 19 activities covering the South Central corridors through Kazipet Junction, Secunderabad, Guntur and Visakhapatnam.

Drive the classic 12703 Falaknuma Express (including the Guntur update build), run the 12723 Telangana Express by ADHI, take the 12438 Hazrat Nizamuddin–Secunderabad Rajdhani by ALOK and the 12285 Secunderabad Duronto by Harsh Sonkusare, or work the coastal sections with the 17015 Visakha Express and 22884 Bhubaneswar–Palasa Express.

Every file downloads as a single archive you can extract and load straight into MSTS or Open Rails — no installer needed.

How to Load TSCR-V2-KZJ Update Activities

Quick 4-step guide to get any Kazipet activity running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

  1. Download the Activity File — hit the Download button on the activity you want in the table below and save the archive to your PC.
  2. Extract — right-click the archive and extract with WinRAR, 7-Zip or Windows Explorer into a folder.
  3. Copy to the Route Folder — move the extracted activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of your TSCR-V2-KZJ route in .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the activity and start your run. Enjoy the session!
