About These Activities

The KERELA V2 Update Acts pack collects the ready-to-run scenario files built for the KERELA V2 update of the MSTS / Open Rails route — 11 activities covering Kerala's express, intercity and passenger services, plus two development builds.

From long-distance expresses like the 20923 Tirunelveli–Gandhidham Humsafar and 12625 Kerala Express to busy intercity runs such as the 16347 TVC-MAQ and 16326 Nilambur Road Intercity, every file downloads as a single RAR you can extract and load straight into MSTS or Open Rails.

How to Load KERELA V2 Update Activities

Quick 4-step guide to get any Kerala activity running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

  1. Download the Activity File — hit the Download button on the activity you want in the table above and save the .rar file to your PC.
  2. Extract — right-click the RAR and extract with WinRAR or 7-Zip into a folder.
  3. Copy to the Route Folder — move the extracted activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of your KERELA V2 route in .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the activity and start your Kerala run. Enjoy the session!
