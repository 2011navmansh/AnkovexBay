About MSTS Environment Sounds

MSTS Environment Sounds is a two-part environment ambience library for Microsoft Train Simulator & Open Rails. Instead of focusing on horns or engines, it builds the world around your train — background ambience, weather air, distant station activity and regional atmosphere that make a route feel alive.

The set is split by terrain: one archive covers hill regions such as Konkan, NFR and other ghat sections, while the second covers non-hill regions such as ER, ECoR and WR. Drop the environment files that match your route and the sim keeps a natural, continuous background bed from departure to destination.

How to Install

  1. Pick a Set — Choose the hill regions or non-hill regions archive below and download it.
  2. Extract — Extract the 7z archive with 7-Zip / WinRAR.
  3. Copy to Sounds — Copy the environment sound files into your route's \SOUND\ folder.
  4. Launch & Listen — Open MSTS or Open Rails and enjoy the ambience along your route.
