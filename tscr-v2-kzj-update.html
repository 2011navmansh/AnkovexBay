About This Update

The TSCR-V2-KZJ Update is the V2 refresh of the TSCR route for Microsoft Train Simulator (MSTS) and Open Rails, centred on Kazipet Junction (KZJ) on the South Central Railway. The update rebuilds the busy junction and its approaches with authentic station detail, fresh trackwork and a full scenery and texture pass.

Kazipet Junction is one of South Central Railway's great crossroads — where the Delhi–Chennai trunk line meets the lines toward Balharshah and Secunderabad. The update captures that constant flow of traffic: long-distance expresses, suburban services and heavy freight sharing the same junction throat.

The route ships as 6 split RAR parts (~9.6 GB): download every part into one folder, then extract Part 1 and the archive merges automatically.

Key Features

  • Kazipet Junction (KZJ) — the SCR crossroads rebuilt with platform, yard and signal-cabin detail.
  • Updated Trackwork — refined track, turnouts and block sections across the junction and its approaches.
  • Fresh Scenery & Textures — a new scenery and texture pass along the SCR mainline.
  • Traffic Variety — long-distance expresses, suburban workings and mixed freight action.
  • MSTS + Open Rails Ready — built for Microsoft Train Simulator and fully compatible with Open Rails on Windows.

How to Install the TSCR-V2-KZJ Update

Quick 4-step guide to get the TSCR-V2-KZJ Update running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails. The download comes as 6 split RAR parts (~9.6 GB).

  1. Download & Extract — download all 6 RAR parts into the same folder, then right-click Part 1 → Extract with WinRAR / 7-Zip (the parts merge automatically).
  2. Copy to Routes Folder — move the extracted route folder inside .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  3. Run Installme.bat — double-click installme.bat inside the route folder to copy required default assets (if included).
  4. Launch & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the TSCR route, and start your session!
