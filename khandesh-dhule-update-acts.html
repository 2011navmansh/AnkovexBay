About These Activities

The Khandesh Dhule Update Acts pack collects the ready-to-run scenario files built for the Khandesh Dhule update of the MSTS / Open Rails route — five activities covering anniversary specials, superfast expresses and the new Dhule section.

Drive the 12124 Deccan Queen 96th Anniversary special, take the 12289 Mumbai CSMT–Nagpur Duronto (Part 2) by rehan Manyar, run 18519 Alok, handle the delayed-start 12493 Darshan SF Express, or work the fresh Dhule to Chalisgaon Junction operations with the New Dhule Update Activity.

How to Load Khandesh Update Activities

Quick 4-step guide to get any Khandesh activity running inside Microsoft Train Simulator or Open Rails.

  1. Download the Activity File — hit the Download button in the table above and save the .rar file to your PC.
  2. Extract — right-click the RAR and extract with WinRAR or 7-Zip into a folder.
  3. Copy to the Route Folder — move the extracted activity files into the ACTIVITIES folder of your Khandesh route in .../Train Simulator/ROUTES/.
  4. Drive & Enjoy — open MSTS or Open Rails, select the activity and start your Khandesh run. Enjoy the session!
