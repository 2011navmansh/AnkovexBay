LCD to OLED Intel Command Center Settings

The complete guide to switching your display from LCD to OLED in the Intel Command Center — with full About, description and 3 screenshots to follow along step by step.

Topic: Display / Panel Switch  ·  Tool: Intel Command Center  ·  Type: Step-by-step guide  ·  Price: Free

desktop_windows

100% Free. No downloads, no payments — pure on-screen help. Everything is done through the Intel Command Center settings.

About — LCD to OLED Intel Command Center Settings

What is this guide? Many modern laptops ship with an Intel panel that can be switched between LCD and OLED display modes through the Intel Command Center (the Intel graphics / display center app). This guide shows you exactly how to open the Command Center, find the display mode setting, switch your panel from LCD to OLED, and save the correct colour profile so the change stays after a reboot.

Who is it for? Anyone with an Intel-powered laptop that has both an LCD and an OLED panel option — including users who upgraded their display and need to tell Windows and Intel which panel to use, and people who simply prefer the richer blacks and punchier colours of OLED. No technical degree needed — everything is done through the on-screen settings.

What you need: A laptop or PC with an Intel graphics chip, the Intel Command Center application installed (usually pre-loaded, or available from the Intel website), and admin access. That's it.

  • Find the setting — Navigate the Intel Command Center to the correct display tab.
  • Switch the panel — Change display mode from LCD to OLED in a couple of clicks.
  • Save profile — Keep your OLED colour profile after a restart.
  • 100% Free — No downloads, no payments — pure on-screen help.
